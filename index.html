Language turns “thinking” itself into an action.

The state is not only the prompt; it includes what the model has already produced and any tool results. An action may be one token, one reasoning span, or an entire response trajectory. The key intuition is that reasoning enters the action space.

SFT often gives the model one reference response. RLVR can sample several responses from the current model, run tests, and make successful trajectories more likely. This reward sees the outcome, so it may miss style, safety, or maintainability.

Chu et al., 2025 · PRISM, 2026

This is the core systems insight. If the model is too weak, successful samples are too rare. Useful mid-training or SFT supplies primitives and format. Overfitting can instead lock down exploration. The stages shape one another's ceiling. [Sources] Chu et al., SFT Memorizes, RL Generalizes. https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.17161 PRISM: Demystifying Retention and Interaction in Mid-Training. https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.17074

Use the comparison to review RL. The better question is: under which data, model, and objective is each method appropriate? REAL QUALITY?

If a verifier checks only “pass,”
the model may learn a shortcut.

01Reward hacking

Maximizing a proxy may miss the real goal

02Sparse reward

A final score cannot identify which reasoning step helped

03Entropy collapse

The policy becomes safer, narrower, and less exploratory

04Compute & stability

Repeated sampling, checking, and updating is costly and noisy

Helff et al., 2026 · DAPO, 2025

RL does not decide whether an objective is what we truly want. It becomes better at optimizing the reward we gave it. RLVR verifiers can contain loopholes, and one final reward creates a credit-assignment problem over a long reasoning chain. [Sources] Helff et al., LLMs Gaming Verifiers. https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.15149 Yu et al., DAPO. https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.14476

The key difference from ordinary distillation is that OPD does not rely only on pre-generated teacher answers. The student moves first, and the teacher responds to states the student actually visits. This combines dense supervision with on-policy data. Two additional directions are better credit assignment and evaluations tied to real utility.
